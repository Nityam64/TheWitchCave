How is the suggested order worked out?

Average daily use over the last 7 days, times 7, gives the normal week. Festival ingredients are multiplied while the festival is on. Then the safety buffer is added and what is in stock is subtracted.

Why can't a sale go through?

Each sale is checked against stock first. If any ingredient is short, nothing changes and the form tells you which one.

What is an unusual stock drop?

Today's use is more than twice the average of the six days before it.

Where is my data saved?

In this browser only, for this prototype. Syncing across web and Android is the next step.
